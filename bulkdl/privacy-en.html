BulkDL Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Japanese version. If the two versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Last updated: October 23, 2026
Operator: Ham's Studio / Contact: info.hamsstudio@gmail.com

1. Our approach

BulkDL (the "App") does not send your personal information or usage data to the operator's servers. The App does not include any analytics SDKs.

The free version uses Google's advertising service "AdMob" to show ads (Section 4). The Pro (paid) version does not show ads.

To process Pro purchases and check subscription status, the App uses "RevenueCat", a service of RevenueCat, Inc. (Section 5). The operator can view purchase status in RevenueCat's dashboard.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information only on your device.

Information Purpose
URLs and titles of pages you selected from the share menu or bookmarks, the download targets, and their progress Showing the list and resuming interrupted downloads
Download history (URL, title, URL of the source page, save location, date and time, reason for failure) Showing the history, opening and sharing saved videos, and avoiding saving the same video twice
Tabs in the in-app browser (URLs and titles of open pages) Reopening the same pages the next time you start the App
In-app bookmarks (URLs, titles and folders, including the contents of bookmark files you import) Showing bookmarks and downloading a folder in bulk
Settings (such as downloading only on Wi-Fi, or saving to the private folder) Keeping your settings
Pro subscription status (the result of the last check) Letting you use Pro features right after startup and when you are offline
Videos in the private folder (Pro) Saving videos where your gallery and other apps cannot see them
The free version's save allowance (free saves used today, remaining save tickets, number of ads watched) and the time an ad was last shown Managing the free version's save allowance and not showing too many ads
Ad block lists (public lists fetched automatically and lists you add) and the date they were last updated Stopping ads from loading
Cookies, site data and cache of the in-app browser Keeping you signed in and displaying pages

This information is excluded from Google Drive backups and from data transfer when you move to a new device.

3. Network connections

All network connections made by the App go directly to the destinations below. They do not pass through the operator's servers, and the operator does not receive their contents.

Connection Destination Main information sent
Fetching pages and videos (browsing in the in-app browser and downloading) Websites you open or select, and the servers that deliver their videos The same information as a regular browser (IP address, device and browser type, cookies, URL of the source page [Referer], etc.)
Checking whether a video can be saved (done for each candidate in the list before downloading) The candidate websites and the servers that deliver their videos Same as above. To check, the App may open the page in a browser that is not shown on screen
Checking video sizes (to choose which video on a page to save) The servers that deliver the videos Same as above. Only the file size or playlist is fetched; the video itself is not downloaded
Fetching thumbnails (to show images in the list; can be turned off in settings) The candidate websites and the servers of the images those pages specify Same as above. Only the beginning of the page's HTML and the image are fetched. Thumbnails of saved videos are made from the video on your device, so no connection is made
Search (when you enter words other than a URL in the address bar) Google (www.google.com) The words you entered and the same information as a regular browser
Showing ads (free version only) Google (AdMob) As described in Section 4
Purchasing Pro and checking subscription status Google Play, RevenueCat As described in Section 5
Updating ad block lists (about once a week on Wi-Fi, or when you tap "Update ad block list now") GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) Only the information sent with any connection, such as your IP address. Pages you viewed, bookmarks and download history are not sent
  • Information sent to these destinations is handled according to each destination's privacy policy.
  • The App can also open unencrypted (http) pages. Such connections may be visible to third parties on the same network.

4. Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads using "Google AdMob", provided by Google LLC. To show ads, AdMob sends the following information directly from your device to Google, which handles it according to Google's privacy policy. The operator does not receive this information.

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android advertising ID)
  • Information such as your IP address, device and app type and version, and language
  • Records of ad-related actions such as ad views and clicks, and records of malfunctions

In regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, the App shows a consent screen before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in "Ad privacy settings" in the settings.

You can delete or reset your advertising ID, and turn off personalized ads, in your device settings (such as "Privacy" > "Ads").

For how Google handles information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Pro purchases (RevenueCat)

Pro (monthly subscription) is purchased through Google Play's billing system. To verify purchases and manage subscription status (whether it is active, renewed or cancelled), the App uses "RevenueCat", provided by RevenueCat, Inc., and sends the following information from your device to RevenueCat. RevenueCat may also receive purchase information from Google Play.

  • An anonymous user ID created for the App (not linked to your name or email address)
  • Purchase information (product, purchase date, expiration date, purchase identifiers, price and currency, etc.)
  • Information such as your IP address, device type, OS and app version, language and country

The operator can view the purchase status for each anonymous user ID in RevenueCat's dashboard. Pages you viewed, bookmarks, download history and saved videos are not sent. For how RevenueCat handles information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Payment information (such as credit cards) is handled by Google; the App, the operator and RevenueCat do not receive it.

6. Saved videos

Saved videos are stored in your device's shared storage ("Download/BulkDL"). Files in shared storage may be accessible to other apps and to photo or file backup services.

Videos you put in the Pro "private folder" are stored in the App's own storage and cannot be accessed by other apps or backup services. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, videos in the private folder are also deleted.

7. Permissions

Permission Purpose
Internet Fetching videos and web pages
View network state For the setting that downloads only on Wi-Fi
Notifications Showing download progress and completion
Foreground service (data sync) Continuing downloads after you close the screen
Advertising ID and ad-related permissions (used by the ad SDK in the free version) Showing ads and measuring their effectiveness
Google Play billing Purchasing Pro
Biometrics (fingerprint, face, etc.) Unlocking the private folder (biometric data is checked by the device and is not received by the App)

8. Deleting information

  • Cookies, site data and cache of the in-app browser can be deleted with "Clear cookies and browsing data".
  • Download history can be deleted one entry at a time or all at once on the history screen (saved videos are kept).
  • In-app bookmarks can be deleted on the bookmarks screen, and tabs by closing them in the tab list.
  • If you clear the App's data in your device settings or uninstall the App, all information stored on your device is deleted (videos saved in "Download/BulkDL" are kept; videos in the private folder are deleted).
  • To have the purchase information held by RevenueCat deleted, please contact the operator.

9. Sharing with third parties

The App does not provide your information to third parties. However, in the free version, AdMob sends information from your device to Google to show ads, as described in Section 4. In addition, to verify Pro purchases, the App sends purchase information to RevenueCat, as described in Section 5.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.
