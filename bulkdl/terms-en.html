1. What the App cannot do

  • The App cannot save YouTube videos.
  • The App cannot save videos protected by DRM or other technical protection measures (such as paid streaming). The App has no function to remove such protection.

2. Prohibited uses

You must not use the App for the following purposes:

  • Infringing copyrights or other rights (for example, saving a video while knowing it was uploaded without the right holder's permission)
  • Violating the terms of use of the site you save videos from
  • Redistributing or publishing saved videos without the right holder's permission

3. Your responsibility

  • It is your responsibility to decide whether you may save a video, in light of each site's terms of use and applicable laws.
  • To the extent permitted by law, the operator is not liable for any damage arising from the use of the App.

4. Pro (monthly subscription)

  • Pro is a monthly subscription. The price is shown on the purchase screen and charged to your Google Play account.
  • The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Google Play subscriptions. After you cancel, you can keep using Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds are handled according to Google Play's policies.
  • When your Pro subscription ends, ads are shown and bulk downloads are limited to the free version's save allowance. You can still watch videos in the private folder and move them back to Downloads, but you cannot add new ones.
  • Videos in the private folder are deleted if you uninstall the App or clear its data. Move any videos you want to keep back to Downloads beforehand.

5. Open source software

The App uses open source software including yt-dlp (Unlicense), Chaquopy (MIT), Python (PSF License) and FFmpeg (LGPL-2.1 or later). You can view the license of each in "Open source licenses" in the App.

The FFmpeg source code and build instructions are available at https://manabuw.github.io/bulkdl/ffmpeg/. Nothing in these terms restricts replacing FFmpeg with a modified version, or the reverse engineering needed to do so.
